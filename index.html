👨‍💻 What is OpenXW?

OpenXW is a free, open-source reimplementation of the legendary 1990s space combat simulator Star Wars: X-Wing. It was rebuilt from scratch to run natively on Windows, macOS, and Linux – no emulators, no virtual machines, no compatibility hassles. It just works.

This project breathes new life into a classic. You get the authentic 1994 experience with CD-quality audio, full-motion cutscenes, and the famous adaptive iMUSE soundtrack, plus the smooth 1998 flight simulation and updated 3D graphics. It's the best of both worlds, combined into one seamless package.
