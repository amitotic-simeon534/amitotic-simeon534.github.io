Selected Builds

Game / Space↗

Starhopper

Fly, land, and take off across four hand-built planets. Twelve original creatures judge your landings: come in soft and they celebrate, come in hot and they remember.

Game / 3D Obby↗

Moonclimb

A low-gravity climb up a moon that does not want you at the top. Coyote time, jump buffering, and platforms tuned against the physics until it feels right.

AI / Voice↗

Coop Voice

A resident listener on the home PC. Say its name, ask out loud, and it answers through the speakers in a proper voice. Whisper-grade ears, zero cloud, all local.

AI / Local Models↗

The Humanizer

A 12-billion-parameter local model that rewrites stiff AI drafts until they read like a person wrote them, with every number, date, and name kept exactly intact.
