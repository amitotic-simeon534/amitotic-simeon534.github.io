A family shop, not a factory

Happy Days Printing started in Marysville in 2024, and every order is still printed, cut, packed and shipped by our small team. Most of what we make starts as somebody's best day: a wedding, a new baby, a dog wearing a birthday hat.

You will also find our booth at local fairs and events around the county, making magnets on the spot. If you have ever seen a line of people comparing fresh magnets at the fair, that was probably us.

Memories fade. Magnets hold. That is the whole idea.

Good questions

How do I personalize my magnets?

Choose a product, upload your photos, then crop, edit and preview before submitting. We print, cut, pack and ship within 2 to 3 business days, with a tracking confirmation on every order.

What sizes do you offer?

Classic magnets are 2″ × 2″ squares; rounds are 2.25″. Flex magnets come in 2″, 2.5″ and 3″ squares plus rectangles, and save the dates go up to 3.75″.

How long until my order arrives?

Production takes 2 to 3 business days. Shipping time then depends on your location, and every order includes tracking.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Orders go straight into production, so we cannot cancel or modify them once placed. Please double-check your photos and details before checkout.

What is your return policy?

Personalized items are final sale, unless your order arrives damaged or we made an error. If anything is wrong, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will make it right.

Do you ship internationally?

Not at this time. We ship within the United States.
