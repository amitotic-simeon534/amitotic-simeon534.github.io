A family shop, not a factory

Happy Days Printing is a family-owned business in Marysville, Washington, started in 2024. Every order is printed, cut, packed and shipped by our small team, most of them from photos of somebody's best day: a wedding, a new baby, a dog in a birthday hat.

You will also find our booth at local fairs and events around the county, making magnets on the spot. If you have seen a line of people holding fresh magnets at the fair, that was probably us.

Good questions

How do I personalize my photo magnets?

Choose your product, upload your favorite photos, then crop, edit and preview them before submitting. When you are happy, add the set to your cart and check out. We print, cut, pack and ship within 2 to 3 business days, and you will get a confirmation with tracking.

What sizes do you offer?

Our classic photo magnets are 2" × 2" squares, and rounds are 2.25". Flexible magnets come in squares of 2", 2.5" and 3" plus rectangles, and save the date magnets go up to 3.75".

How long until my order arrives?

Production takes 2 to 3 business days, then shipping time depends on your location. Every order ships with a tracking number.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Orders go straight into production, so we cannot cancel or modify them once placed. Please double-check your photos and details before checkout, and email us right away if something looks wrong.

What is your return policy?

Because every item is personalized, we do not accept returns or refunds unless your item arrives damaged or we made an error. If anything is wrong, contact us within 7 days of receiving your package and we will make it right.

Do you ship internationally?

Not at this time. We ship within the United States.

How do event packages work?

Book with a $50 deposit to reserve your date. Our team brings the booth, your guests upload photos from their phones with a QR code, and we make the magnets on site so guests take them home the same day. Packages run from $225 to $875, with extra magnets at $3 each and extra hours at $50.
