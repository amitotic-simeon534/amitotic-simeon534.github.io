Survival Intel

// field notes, author unknown
NOTE 01 · POINTS

Rounds 1 to 4 are farming, not fighting. Every hit pays 10, every board you rebuild pays 10. A rich round five beats a heroic round two, here and everywhere.

NOTE 02 · BOARDS

In this room the boards are the game. A window with three planks is a savings account. A window with none is a debt collector standing in your kitchen.

NOTE 03 · SPENDING

Juggernog first, always. Then whatever keeps you alive longest. The Pack-a-Punch is a luxury purchased by people whose windows are already safe.
