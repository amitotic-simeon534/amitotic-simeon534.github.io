Survival Intel

// field notes, author unknown
NOTE 01 · POINTS

Rounds 1 to 4 are farming, not fighting. Knife the early ones, shoot legs before heads, and let the barricades earn their rebuild points. A rich round five beats a heroic round two.

NOTE 02 · THE TRAIN

Keep them in a herd, keep the herd moving, and never stop sprinting in a straight line. The moment you check your points mid-train, the train checks you.

NOTE 03 · THE CRAWLER

Always leave a crawler at the end of a round. It is the difference between a calm shopping trip and buying Juggernog while the ceiling comes down. Guard him with your life. He is the pause button.
